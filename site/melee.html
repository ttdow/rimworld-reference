    BEFORE YOU PICK A WINNER

    What the model knows

    The comparison

    A healthy adult human begins each fight with the same starting settings. The attacker stays in range; the target dodges but never retaliates. Time starts with a ready attack at 0 s, so a one-swing incapacitation takes 0 s. The fight ends at the first death, pain shock, consciousness below 30%, or moving at or below 15%. Temporary stun is not an endpoint.

    “Dead first” is the fraction of all trials that end in death before downing. It does not predict eventual survival after downing. The model has no storyteller death-on-down roll.

    The wiki inputs

    Attack selection uses the wiki’s Best/Mid/Worst rule, including natural attacks. Damage varies from 80–120%. Layered armor rolls can block damage or halve it and convert sharp damage to blunt. Injuries affect part HP, pain, movement, and consciousness; dodge changes as the target is injured.

    All target apparel is Normal quality at fixed protection. Flak and marine presets include a cloth button-down shirt and pants. Fingers and feet remain exposed. A steel simple helmet leaves the face exposed.

    Where this is approximate

    No bleeding progression, healing, ongoing fire, movement, retaliation, armor degradation, implants, genes, persona traits, or procedural unique weapons. Plasma swords include a simplified direct heat proc; fire and panic are omitted, so their ranking is incomplete. EMP has no effect on this natural human. Beer and wood do not break or get consumed.

    Capacity aggregation, missing-part pain, adjacent hit selection, and fractional damage are approximations. Scratch behavior is itself marked uncertain on the wiki. Brain-scar pain and game tick rounding are omitted. Confidence intervals cannot account for these model differences.

    Read the statistics

    Unfinished trials are right-censored at the selected time limit. Percentiles include every trial. Not reached means too few targets were incapacitated to observe that percentile. Median intervals use binomial order-statistic bounds; probability intervals use Wilson bounds, both at 95%.

    Use a small run to explore, then increase trials for a narrow weapon pool. Overlapping intervals call for more evidence. Different armor presets are separate experiments; this page does not combine them into an overall winner.
