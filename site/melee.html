    Model and sources

    • First swing: 0 s. Each trial starts with a fresh human target.
    • Incapacitation: death, pain shock, consciousness <30%, or moving ≤15%. Stun does not count.
    • Timeouts remain in all percentiles. “Not reached” means the percentile exceeds the time limit.
    • 95% intervals measure sampling uncertainty, not model accuracy.
    • “Dead first” counts deaths before downing, divided by all trials.
    • Fixed = no quality variation. No material = no material variation. Unarmed is selected separately.
